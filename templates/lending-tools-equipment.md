# Lending tools or equipment

Use this when lending a trailer, ladder, pressure washer, tool kit or similar to a friend or a neighbour.

How to use it: fill in every [BRACKET]. Where a bracket shows choices split by a slash, keep one. Write money as numbers and dates as dates. Delete any optional clause you do not need.

## Short form

One message. Send it and keep the reply.

> All yours for [HOW LONG]. Sent you [NUMBER] photos of it this morning. If anything breaks, we split the repair. Back by [RETURN DAY AND TIME]? Reply yes so we both have it in writing.

## Full form

- **Owner:** [OWNER NAME], [OWNER PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Borrower:** [BORROWER NAME], [BORROWER PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Date agreed:** [DATE AGREED]

### 1. What we're doing

[OWNER NAME] lends [BORROWER NAME] [ITEM], with [ACCESSORIES], for [WHAT IT WILL BE USED FOR]. Its condition at handover is shown in the photos sent on [DATE]. Only [BORROWER NAME] uses it. It is not lent on to anyone else.

### 2. Price and payment

Cost: [FREE / AMOUNT PER DAY OR WEEKEND]. Deposit: [NONE / AMOUNT, RETURNED WHEN IT COMES BACK]. Fuel and consumables: [WHO PAYS].

### 3. Changes

To keep it longer, [BORROWER NAME] asks before [RETURN DAY AND TIME], and we agree on the new time in a message. Each change is a new message, not an edit.

### 4. Dates

Pick-up [DAY AND TIME] at [PLACE]. Back by [RETURN DAY AND TIME] at [PLACE].

### 5. If someone walks

If it breaks or is damaged while borrowed, [WE SPLIT THE REPAIR / THE BORROWER PAYS FOR THE REPAIR]. Normal wear: [WHAT COUNTS AS NORMAL WEAR, AND WHO PAYS]. If it is lost or stolen, [WHAT HAPPENS, FOR EXAMPLE THE BORROWER PAYS AMOUNT]. Either of us can end the loan early. It comes back within [NUMBER] days.

### Agreed

- [OWNER NAME], [DATE]
- [BORROWER NAME], [DATE]

Each of us keeps a copy.

## Optional clauses

Add any that fit, above "Agreed". Each one stands alone.

- **Photos at return too.** We take the same photos when it comes back.
- **Cleaning.** It comes back [CLEAN / AS IT IS, AND THE OWNER CLEANS IT FOR AMOUNT].
- **How to use it.** [OWNER NAME] shows how it works before handover. Safety gear: [SAFETY GEAR].
- **Trailer or vehicle.** Tires, lights and hitch are checked together at pick-up and at return.
- **Fuel.** Full when it leaves, full when it comes back.
- **Borrower sends the photos.** [BORROWER NAME] sends the photos before pick-up, and [OWNER NAME] replies to confirm they show the condition.

## Example

> All yours for the weekend. Sent you two photos of it this morning. If anything breaks, we split the repair. Back by Sunday at 6? Reply yes so we both have it in writing.

## Check the rules where you live

If the item is a trailer, vehicle or powered equipment, check your insurance and the road and licensing rules where you live before you lend it. Your insurer can tell you whether someone else using it is covered.
