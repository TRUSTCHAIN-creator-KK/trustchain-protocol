# Selling a used vehicle privately

Use this when a private seller sells a car, truck, motorcycle or trailer to a friend or a stranger.

How to use it: fill in every [BRACKET]. Where a bracket shows choices split by a slash, keep one. Write money as numbers and dates as dates. Delete any optional clause you do not need.

## Short form

One message. Send it and keep the reply.

> [PRICE] for the [VEHICLE, FOR EXAMPLE 2012 PICKUP], paid in full by [PAYMENT DATE]. It runs fine today, but [KNOWN ISSUES]. Sold as is. Reply yes if that's the deal.

## Full form

- **Seller:** [SELLER NAME], [SELLER PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Buyer:** [BUYER NAME], [BUYER PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Date agreed:** [DATE AGREED]

### 1. What we're doing

[SELLER NAME] sells [BUYER NAME] a [YEAR] [MAKE] [MODEL], VIN [VIN], odometer [READING] on [DATE OF READING]. Included: [INCLUDED ITEMS, FOR EXAMPLE SECOND KEY, WINTER TIRES]. Known issues the seller has told the buyer about: [KNOWN ISSUES]. Sold as is: after handover, repairs are the buyer's to pay for.

### 2. Price and payment

Price [PRICE]. Deposit [DEPOSIT AMOUNT] paid on [DEPOSIT DATE]. Balance [BALANCE AMOUNT] paid by [PAYMENT METHOD] by [PAYMENT DATE]. Keys and papers are handed over when the price is paid in full.

### 3. Changes

If either of us finds a problem before handover, we either agree on a new price in writing or end the deal under line 5. Each change is a new note, not an edit.

### 4. Dates

Test drive or inspection by [INSPECTION DATE]. Handover at [PLACE] on [HANDOVER DATE]. Transfer paperwork is done on [DATE], the same day as handover.

### 5. If someone walks

If the buyer backs out after paying the deposit, [THE DEPOSIT IS KEPT / THE DEPOSIT IS RETURNED / PART OF THE DEPOSIT IS RETURNED]. If the seller backs out, the deposit is returned within [NUMBER] days. If the balance is not paid by [PAYMENT DATE], [WHAT HAPPENS, FOR EXAMPLE THE SELLER MAY SELL TO SOMEONE ELSE AND RETURNS THE DEPOSIT].

### Agreed

- [SELLER NAME], [DATE]
- [BUYER NAME], [DATE]

Each of us keeps a copy.

## Optional clauses

Add any that fit, above "Agreed". Each one stands alone.

- **Inspection.** The buyer may have the vehicle checked by a mechanic of their choice before [INSPECTION DATE], at the buyer's cost.
- **Money owed on the vehicle.** The seller states that [NO MONEY IS OWED ON THE VEHICLE / AMOUNT IS OWED TO LENDER AND IS PAID OFF FROM THE PRICE AT HANDOVER].
- **Fees and taxes.** Transfer fees and taxes are paid by [BUYER / SELLER / SPLIT].
- **Plates and insurance.** [WHAT HAPPENS TO THE PLATES]. The seller's insurance ends at handover.
- **Tickets and tolls.** Tickets, tolls and fines from the handover time on are the buyer's.
- **Paying in parts.** The balance is paid in [NUMBER] parts: [AMOUNTS AND DATES]. Keys and papers are handed over [AT THE START / AFTER THE LAST PAYMENT].

## Example

> $4,500 for the truck, paid in full by Nov 1. It runs fine today, but the rear brakes need doing soon. Sold as is. Reply yes if that's the deal.

## Check the rules where you live

Transfer forms, taxes, safety or emissions checks, and how to check whether money is owed on a vehicle differ by province, state and country. Check with your vehicle licensing office before handover. What "as is" covers can also depend on local rules. Do the transfer paperwork the same day as handover.
