# Personal loan between friends

Use this when one person lends another money, informally, with a plan to pay it back.

How to use it: fill in every [BRACKET]. Where a bracket shows choices split by a slash, keep one. Write money as numbers and dates as dates. Delete any optional clause you do not need.

## Short form

One message. Send it and keep the reply.

> Happy to. [LOAN AMOUNT] now. Can you send [FIRST PAYMENT] back on [FIRST DATE] and [SECOND PAYMENT] on [SECOND DATE]? Just reply yes so we both have the dates.

## Full form

- **Lender:** [LENDER NAME], [LENDER PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Borrower:** [BORROWER NAME], [BORROWER PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Date agreed:** [DATE AGREED]

### 1. What we're doing

[LENDER NAME] lends [BORROWER NAME] [LOAN AMOUNT] [CURRENCY], sent by [HOW, FOR EXAMPLE BANK TRANSFER] on [TRANSFER DATE]. This is a loan, not a gift.

### 2. Price and payment

[BORROWER NAME] pays it back like this: [REPAYMENT SCHEDULE, FOR EXAMPLE AMOUNT ON DATE AND AMOUNT ON DATE], by [HOW]. Interest: [NONE / THE RATE AND HOW IT IS WORKED OUT].

### 3. Changes

If a date will not work, [BORROWER NAME] says so before that date, and we agree on a new date in writing. Paying early is always fine. Each change is a new message, not an edit.

### 4. Dates

Money sent [TRANSFER DATE]. Payments due [PAYMENT DATES]. Fully repaid by [FINAL DATE].

### 5. If someone walks

If a payment is missed, [LENDER NAME] sends one reminder, and we set a new date within [NUMBER] days. We both agree the unpaid amount is still owed until it is paid, or until we both agree otherwise in writing.

### Agreed

- [LENDER NAME], [DATE]
- [BORROWER NAME], [DATE]

Each of us keeps a copy.

## Optional clauses

Add any that fit, above "Agreed". Each one stands alone.

- **Reminder wording agreed up front.** If a date passes, the reminder will read: "Hey, [DATE] came and went. Still good for this week?"
- **Payment log.** Each payment is confirmed in this thread with the amount and the date.
- **Hard times.** If [BORROWER NAME] cannot pay for a while, we talk before the next date and write down a new plan.
- **Something held until repaid.** [LENDER NAME] keeps [ITEM] until the loan is repaid, then returns it within [NUMBER] days.
- **Paying off early.** Paying off the rest early is fine at any time, with no extra cost.

## Example

> Happy to. $800 now. Can you send $400 back on Oct 30 and $400 on Nov 13? Just reply yes so we both have the dates.

## Check the rules where you live

Rules about charging interest, how long an unpaid loan can be claimed, and what a record of a loan should show can differ where you live. If you add interest, hold something until the loan is repaid, or lend a large amount, check the rules or ask a local professional first. Lend what you could live without.

Not legal advice.
