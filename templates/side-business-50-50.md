# 50/50 side business between friends

Use this when two friends start a small side business together: pressure washing, snow clearing, a weekend crew.

How to use it: fill in every [BRACKET]. Where a bracket shows choices split by a slash, keep one. Write money as numbers and dates as dates. Delete any optional clause you do not need. Agree on the way out while you both still like the plan.

## Short form

One message. Send it and keep the reply.

> Let's put it in one text: [START MONEY] each to start, profit split 50/50 every [MONTH / WEEK]. If one of us quits, the other buys their half of the gear. Reply yes if that's the deal.

## Full form

- **Partner A:** [PARTNER A NAME], [PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Partner B:** [PARTNER B NAME], [PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Date agreed:** [DATE AGREED]

### 1. What we're doing

[PARTNER A NAME] and [PARTNER B NAME] run [WHAT THE BUSINESS DOES] [UNDER THE NAME BUSINESS NAME / WITHOUT A BUSINESS NAME YET], starting [START DATE]. [PARTNER A NAME] brings [WHAT PARTNER A BRINGS]. [PARTNER B NAME] brings [WHAT PARTNER B BRINGS]. Hours each: [EXPECTED HOURS]. Gear is owned half and half.

### 2. Price and payment

Each of us puts in [START MONEY] by [DATE]. The money is kept in [WHERE, FOR EXAMPLE A SHARED ACCOUNT]. Profit means money in minus [WHAT COUNTS AS COSTS]. It is split 50/50 every [MONTH / WEEK] on [DAY]. [WHO] keeps the books and shares them every [HOW OFTEN].

### 3. Changes

Both of us agree in writing before: spending over [AMOUNT], changing prices, borrowing, adding a partner, [OTHER BIG DECISIONS]. Changing the split itself is a new written agreement, not an edit to this one.

### 4. Dates

Start [START DATE]. Profit split every [MONTH / WEEK]. We review this agreement on [REVIEW DATE].

### 5. If someone walks

If one of us quits, they give [NUMBER] weeks' notice. The other buys their half of the gear at [HOW THE PRICE IS SET, FOR EXAMPLE HALF OF WHAT IT WOULD SELL FOR] within [NUMBER] days, or we sell the gear and split the money. Jobs already booked: [WHO FINISHES THEM AND WHO GETS PAID]. The name and the customer list: [WHO KEEPS THEM]. If neither of us wants to continue, we sell the gear, pay what the business owes, and split what is left 50/50.

### Agreed

- [PARTNER A NAME], [DATE]
- [PARTNER B NAME], [DATE]

Each of us keeps a copy.

## Optional clauses

Add any that fit, above "Agreed". Each one stands alone.

- **Unequal hours.** If one of us works more than [NUMBER] hours more in a [MONTH / WEEK], those hours are paid at [HOURLY RATE] before the split.
- **Cannot work for a while.** If one of us cannot work for more than [HOW LONG], we talk and write down a new split for that time.
- **Disagreements.** If we cannot agree, we talk it through first, then ask [A PERSON YOU BOTH TRUST] to help before anything else.
- **Insurance.** [WHO] arranges insurance for the gear and the work.
- **Using the gear for yourself.** Personal use of the gear: [ALLOWED / NOT ALLOWED / ALLOWED IF THE OTHER AGREES FIRST].

## Example

> Let's put it in one text: $1,500 each to start, profit split 50/50 every month. If one of us quits, the other buys their half of the gear. Reply yes if that's the deal.

## Check the rules where you live

Registering a business name, licences, insurance, who is responsible for what the business owes, and how a shared business is taxed depend on where you live and how you set it up. Talk to an accountant or a local business advisor before the first paid job. Get local advice first if either of you is putting in more than you could afford to lose, or if you want a non-compete.
