# Small renovation or repair job

Use this when a homeowner hires a trade for a job you can describe in a few lines.

How to use it: fill in every [BRACKET]. Where a bracket shows choices split by a slash, keep one. Write money as numbers and dates as dates. Delete any optional clause you do not need.

## Short form

One message. Send it and keep the reply.

> Scope: [WORK DESCRIPTION]. [TOTAL PRICE] [PLUS TAX / TAX INCLUDED], [DEPOSIT AMOUNT] before I start, the rest on completion. Anything outside that gets quoted in writing first. Start [START DATE], done by [FINISH DATE]. If either of us stops early, you pay for work done and materials bought, and any unused deposit comes back within [NUMBER] days. Reply yes if that's the deal.

## Full form

- **Client:** [CLIENT NAME], [CLIENT PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Tradesperson:** [TRADESPERSON NAME], [BUSINESS NAME IF ANY], [TRADESPERSON PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Date agreed:** [DATE AGREED]

### 1. What we're doing

I will [WORK DESCRIPTION] at [SITE ADDRESS]. Materials are supplied by [WHO SUPPLIES MATERIALS]. Included: [INCLUDED ITEMS]. Not included: [EXCLUDED ITEMS].

### 2. Price and payment

The price is [TOTAL PRICE] [PLUS TAX / TAX INCLUDED]. It is [A FIXED PRICE / AN ESTIMATE THAT CAN CHANGE BY UP TO AMOUNT]. [DEPOSIT AMOUNT] is due before the start, [MILESTONE PAYMENT] when [MILESTONE], and [FINAL PAYMENT] on completion and walkthrough. Payment is by [PAYMENT METHOD] within [NUMBER] days of each invoice.

### 3. Changes

Any work outside the scope above is quoted separately. It starts only after you confirm the quote in writing. Extra hours are [HOURLY RATE] per hour plus materials. Each change is written down as its own note. This text is not edited.

### 4. Dates

Start [START DATE]. Expected completion [FINISH DATE]. Delays from [DELAY CAUSES, FOR EXAMPLE WEATHER OR LATE MATERIALS] push the completion date by the same number of working days. I will tell you [HOW SOON] when that happens.

### 5. If someone walks

If either of us ends the job early, you pay for work completed and materials bought up to that date. Any unused deposit is refunded within [NUMBER] days.

### Agreed

- [CLIENT NAME], [DATE]
- [TRADESPERSON NAME], [DATE]

Each of us keeps a copy.

## Optional clauses

Add any that fit, above "Agreed". Each one stands alone.

- **Materials as a range.** Materials on top: [MATERIALS LIST], about [LOW AMOUNT] to [HIGH AMOUNT], at cost with receipts.
- **Allowance.** [ITEM] allowance: [AMOUNT]. If your pick costs more, you pay the difference at cost before I order. If it costs less, it comes off the final invoice.
- **Parts you supply.** Please have [PART] on site by [DATE]. If it arrives late, damaged or with missing pieces, the install moves and an extra trip is [TRIP FEE]. The part itself is between you and the seller.
- **Leftover materials.** Unused materials you paid for are yours. Unopened boxes can go back for credit.
- **Tear-out and haul-away.** Tear-out, haul-away and dump fees: [INCLUDED / EXTRA AT AMOUNT].
- **Permits.** [WHO APPLIES] applies for the permit and books inspections. The fee, about [PERMIT FEE], is billed [HOW].
- **Weather.** If it rains on a [OUTDOOR TASK] day, we move to the next dry day and the finish date moves with it. I will text you by [TIME].
- **Quiet hours.** No loud work before [TIME]. Loud work only [HOURS]. That adds [NUMBER] days to the finish date.
- **Access and house rules.** I am in from [WORK HOURS]. [HOUSE RULES]. I will text you a photo of the locked door when I leave.
- **Missed visit.** If nobody is home and I cannot get in at the booked time, the trip is [NO-SHOW FEE] and we rebook.
- **Rescheduling.** If the start moves more than once, the slot goes to the next job on my list and we pick a new date after that.
- **After-hours rate.** Evening, weekend and holiday hours are [AFTER-HOURS RATE] instead of [HOURLY RATE].
- **Who does the work.** [HELPER OR SUBCONTRACTOR] does [TASKS]. Same price, and I check that work before I start mine.
- **Who can approve changes.** Any change, even a small one, goes through [NAME] in writing first.
- **Edges of the trade.** My price covers [WHAT IS INCLUDED AT THE EDGES]. [WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED] is not included.
- **Client does part of the work.** [CLIENT TASK] is yours, done by [DATE]. If it is not done by then, my crew finishes it at [HOURLY RATE] per hour. Damage during your part is fixed as extra work.
- **Taking over someone else's job.** I start from [HANDOVER POINT]. [EARLIER WORK] was done before me and is not part of my work. If I see a problem with it, I will tell you before I build over it.
- **Defining done.** After the walkthrough I send one list. I finish it by [DATE], and the final invoice is due when it is done. New items after [DATE] are a new job.
- **Hourly instead of fixed.** [HOURLY RATE] per hour on site, from when I arrive. Supply runs are [FEE] a trip. I will text you the hours each evening.
- **Neighbour's space.** We use [AREA] on [DAYS], [HOURS]. We protect it, and if we damage anything we fix it or pay for it.
- **Photos of the finished job.** I may post photos of the finished work: no house number, no faces, nothing from the street.

## Example

> Scope: tile the kitchen backsplash, about 40 sq ft, tile supplied by you. $3,200 plus tax, $960 before I start, the rest on completion. Anything outside that gets quoted in writing first. Start Monday June 8, done by Friday June 12. If either of us stops early, you pay for work done and materials bought, and any unused deposit comes back within 7 days. Reply yes if that's the deal.

## Check the rules where you live

There may be local rules about permits, trade licences, deposits, and what either side can do if payment or work stops. Check them with your local building office or a professional before work starts. This template is a plain-words starting point. It does not say what the law is anywhere.
