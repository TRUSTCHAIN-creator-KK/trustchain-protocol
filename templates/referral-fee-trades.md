# Referral fee between trades

Use this when one trade sends a client to another and a thank-you payment is expected.

How to use it: fill in every [BRACKET]. Where a bracket shows choices split by a slash, keep one. Write money as numbers and dates as dates. Delete any optional clause you do not need.

## Short form

One message. Send it and keep the reply.

> Thanks for sending [CLIENT NAME] my way. If the [JOB, FOR EXAMPLE FENCE] goes ahead, I'll send you [FEE] once they've paid the final invoice. Reply yes if that works for you.

## Full form

- **Referrer:** [REFERRER NAME], [BUSINESS NAME IF ANY], [PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Tradesperson:** [TRADESPERSON NAME], [BUSINESS NAME IF ANY], [PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Date agreed:** [DATE AGREED]

### 1. What we're doing

[REFERRER NAME] introduced [CLIENT NAME] to [TRADESPERSON NAME] for [JOB DESCRIPTION] on [REFERRAL DATE]. The fee covers [THIS JOB ONLY / ANY JOB FOR THIS CLIENT BOOKED BY DATE].

### 2. Price and payment

Fee: [AMOUNT / PERCENT OF THE FINAL INVOICE BEFORE TAX], paid by [PAYMENT METHOD] within [NUMBER] days after the client pays in full.

### 3. Changes

If the job grows or shrinks, the fee [STAYS THE SAME / FOLLOWS THE FINAL INVOICE]. Any change to the fee is agreed in writing before the job starts. A change is a new note, not an edit to this one.

### 4. Dates

Referral made [REFERRAL DATE]. Fee due [NUMBER] days after the client's final payment. Covers jobs booked by [DATE].

### 5. If someone walks

If the job does not go ahead, or the client does not pay, no fee is owed. If the client pays only part, [NO FEE / THE SAME SHARE OF THE FEE]. Either of us can stop future referrals at any time. Fees already earned are still paid.

### Agreed

- [REFERRER NAME], [DATE]
- [TRADESPERSON NAME], [DATE]

Each of us keeps a copy.

## Optional clauses

Add any that fit, above "Agreed". Each one stands alone.

- **Who stands behind the work.** [TRADESPERSON NAME] alone is responsible for the work and for dealing with the client.
- **Telling the client.** We [WILL / WILL NOT] tell the client about the fee.
- **Both directions.** The same terms apply when [TRADESPERSON NAME] sends work to [REFERRER NAME].
- **No-fee version.** Send this instead of the template: "I don't do referral fees, but dinner's on me."
- **Work swap instead of a fee.** Swap: I do [JOB A], [MATERIALS A] included. You do [JOB B], [MATERIALS B] included. We call it even. If either job grows, we say so first.

## Example

> Thanks for sending the Hendersons my way. If the fence goes ahead, I'll send you $150 once they've paid the final invoice. Reply yes if that works for you.

## Check the rules where you live

Some trades, licensed professions and regulated sales such as real estate, insurance or finance may have rules about referral payments and about telling the client. Check what applies to your licence, and ask your accountant how to report referral fees.
