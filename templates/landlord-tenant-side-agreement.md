# Landlord and tenant side agreement

Use this when a landlord and a tenant agree on a change outside the lease: painting, an extra occupant, a pet, yard work for lower rent, parking, or a sublet.

How to use it: fill in every [BRACKET]. Where a bracket shows choices split by a slash, keep one. Write money as numbers and dates as dates. Delete any optional clause you do not need. If the rent changes, write the new amount: "a bit less" is not a rent. Keep this with the lease.

## Short form

One message. Send it and keep the reply.

> Yes to [THE CHANGE, FOR EXAMPLE PAINTING THE BEDROOM] [ANY CONDITIONS]. [WHAT HAPPENS AT THE END OR AT MOVE-OUT]. Reply yes and I'll keep this with the lease.

## Full form

- **Landlord:** [LANDLORD NAME], [LANDLORD PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Tenant:** [TENANT NAME], [TENANT PHONE OR EMAIL]
- **Rental address:** [RENTAL ADDRESS]
- **Lease this goes with:** dated [LEASE DATE], names as on the lease
- **Date agreed:** [DATE AGREED]

### 1. What we're doing

What changes: [THE CHANGE]. The tenant gets: [WHAT THE TENANT GETS]. The tenant gives: [WHAT THE TENANT GIVES]. Everything else in the lease stays the same.

### 2. Price and payment

Rent: [NO CHANGE / FROM DATE THE RENT IS AMOUNT].

### 3. Changes

This side agreement changes only if both of us agree in writing. A change is a new note, not an edit to this one.

### 4. Dates

Agreed on [DATE AGREED]. Starts [START DATE]. Ends [END DATE / WHEN SOMETHING HAPPENS, FOR EXAMPLE WHEN THE YARD WORK STOPS].

### 5. If someone walks

Either of us can end this side agreement by [HOW, FOR EXAMPLE A WRITTEN NOTE WITH NUMBER DAYS NOTICE]. After it ends, [WHAT GOES BACK, FOR EXAMPLE THE RENT RETURNS TO AMOUNT]. At move-out: [WHAT HAPPENS AT MOVE-OUT].

### Agreed

- [LANDLORD NAME], [DATE]
- [TENANT NAME], [DATE]

Each of us keeps a copy with the lease.

## Optional clauses

Add any that fit, above "Agreed". Each one stands alone.

- **Painting or small changes.** Room: [ROOM]. Colours: [COLOURS]. At move-out: [LEFT AS IS / BACK TO THE CURRENT COLOUR, AT WHOSE COST].
- **Extra occupant.** [OCCUPANT NAME] stays from [START DATE] to [END DATE]. Rent: [NO CHANGE / AMOUNT].
- **Work for rent.** The tenant does [THE WORK] every [HOW OFTEN]. Rent is [REDUCED RENT] while that continues. If it stops, rent goes back to [ORIGINAL RENT] from [WHEN].
- **Parking or storage.** [SPOT OR SPACE] [IS ADDED / IS REMOVED]. Rent: [NO CHANGE / AMOUNT].
- **Pet.** [PET DESCRIPTION] allowed from [DATE]. [CONDITIONS].
- **Sublet.** The room is [SUBTENANT NAME]'s from [START DATE] to [END DATE]. Rent [AMOUNT], sent to [WHO] by [DAY OF THE MONTH]. [WHAT HAPPENS TO BELONGINGS LEFT IN THE ROOM]. Anything broken during the stay, [WHO PAYS]. The landlord has agreed to the sublet in writing.
- **Shared bills between roommates.** [BILLS] are in [NAME]'s name. The total is posted on [DAY], and each person sends [SHARE] by [DAY]. Anyone moving out pays their share up to the day they leave.

## Example

> Yes to painting the bedroom any light colour. Back to the current white at move-out, at your cost. Reply yes and I'll keep this with the lease.

A sublet line could read: "The room is Sam's from Nov 1 to Jan 31. Rent $900, sent to me by the 1st."

## Check the rules where you live

Rental rules where you live may limit what a side agreement can change, even if you both agree and both sign. Areas worth checking first: rent, entry to the unit, services like parking or storage, extra occupants, and deposits. Ask your local tenancy office or a legal clinic before you rely on it. A written side agreement records what you both said. It does not decide whether a term is allowed.
